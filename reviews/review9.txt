## The biggest revision I would make conceptually

The paper is getting close to having a much better scientific question than “can we make a fast ZDC generator?”

That question is:

> **Where does an explicitly constrained hierarchical generator lose the joint structure of calorimeter showers despite getting local or marginal quantities approximately right?**

Your present numbers already hint at a compelling answer:

* same number of active layers, wrong sequence arrangement;
* exact number of selected channels, wrong connectivity;
* exact generated-energy closure, wrong ECAL/HCAL allocation;
* lower teacher-forced scalar objective, disastrous free-running zero law.

But right now those are **suggestive coincidences** rather than a controlled scientific demonstration.

The oracle cascade, sequence-correlation analysis, support diagnostics, loss decomposition, and common-bank controls are what would turn this from an impressively audited development report into a potentially interesting paper.

And before any of that, fix the **4,390/3,990 ganging error and investigate the hidden upper-cap atom**. Those are the two nastiest concrete issues I found in this continuation.
