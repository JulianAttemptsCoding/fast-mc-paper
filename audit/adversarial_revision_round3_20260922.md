# Adversarial review, current revision

The two user-supplied reviews were evaluated as critiques, not directions to change evidence. They identified one factual error in v0.8.0: the paper said no paired uncertainty was available for the zero-deposit difference. The immutable report contains a paired, primary-energy-stratified 1,000-resample bootstrap with a 95% percentile interval of 0.0019–0.0076 for generated minus reference zero fractions. The evaluator source computes the statistic on common resampled pair indices. The manuscript now reports this interval and limits the missing-uncertainty statement to structural metrics. Repeated examination of the bank rules out a confirmatory reading of the interval.

The central structural correction is geometric. The model graph has longitudinal edges only between adjacent layers. A contiguous empty-layer run between occupied layers separates the occupied graph, regardless of lateral topology. The report gives the number of inactive layers within the first-to-last occupied span, not the number of such runs or the component locations. Consequently, the +35.97 mean-component difference cannot be attributed to independent within-layer fragmentation. The title, abstract, results, discussion, conclusion, claim register, and QA guards now distinguish the observed longitudinal reach and gaps from graph components. No topology significance interval was manufactured from aggregates.

The original report's shower-aware classifier values are omitted because the evaluator's row-wise random train/test split can divide a matched reference/generated pair across folds. A separately retained condition-only AUROC of 0.500 checks paired-condition handling but says nothing about shower fidelity. The two split hashes refer to pilot and canonical manifests; the preparation cross-tabulation places pilot training inside canonical training, but the exact pilot-validation/bank overlap remains unresolved. Source-file hashes describe the locally audited code; the source tree was dirty, so they are not proof of the training-time bytes.
