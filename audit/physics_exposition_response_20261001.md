## Claims not adopted

- **M4 significance argument is invalid.** Conditional independence does not establish nonnegative covariance after mixing energy and direction. Bin-average trends cannot determine the covariance at full condition c. More fundamentally, an upper bound SE <= 0.068 GeV cannot imply a 0.045 GeV difference is below one actual SE: SE=0.005 also satisfies the bound and would imply nine SE. Marginal binomial estimates and inverse-square-root Wasserstein scaling are not substitutes for paired, separately nonempty or matched-size distributions. No unsupported SE column or nonsignificance claim was added.
- **M6 conservatism is not guaranteed.** Selecting teacher-forced validation loss does not monotonically improve every free-running support observable. Expected overlap under assumed uniform sampling would not recover actual event identities. Reuse remains disclosed without a guaranteed conservative interpretation.
- **M2 physics-versus-artifact dichotomy is too strong.** Similar energy-weighted centroids would not show all occupancy differences are physically irrelevant; a mismatch would not alone establish detector performance. Threshold and reconstruction studies remain necessary.
- **M3 reference masks are not contiguous.** The reference gap fraction is already 52.43%. Teacher-forcing mismatch is plausible, but the supplied claim that the reference masks are contiguous is false. A full-mask oracle is prioritized in the discussion without attributing a measured causal fraction to activity.
- **Co-occupancy normalization is not a localization test.** Dividing by squared global mean occupancy assumes homogeneous occupancy and neglects node degree and marginal spatial variation. Similar crude lifts cannot locate a discrepancy in deep layers. Per-layer controlled studies require unavailable event-level information.
- **Gumbel sampling distinction.** Ideal, unclipped Gumbel top-K induces successive weighted sampling, distinct from independent Bernoulli inclusion conditioned on a count. The implementation clips uniform draws, and its BCE logits are not established calibrated probabilities. The manuscript retains the calibration concern without representing a different sampling law as a demonstrated correction.
- **FP32 tolerance.** A few-ULP comparison is a useful numerical diagnostic, not evidence of a predeclared tolerance or that the earlier failure did not occur. No guard, tolerance or historical failure was erased.
