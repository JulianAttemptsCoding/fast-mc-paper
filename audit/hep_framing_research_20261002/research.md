# HEP audience research

## APS Style Basics

[Titles and Abstracts](https://journals.aps.org/authors/style-basics). Titles should identify the subject without promotional wording. Abstracts must stand alone and avoid unexplained abbreviations.

Application: Name neutron showers in the title; state the target and comparison immediately; spell out the classifier metric.

## IOP Article Structure

[Abstract, Discussion, Conclusion](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/structure-and-format-of-your-journal-article/). Summarize method, principal results and implications; the discussion interprets results in context and the conclusion identifies the contribution and follow-up. General guidance suggests an abstract no longer than 300 words.

Application: Use a sub-300-word abstract; open the discussion with the meaning of the discrepancy; end with a bounded validation lesson. This is guidance, not a claimed target-journal requirement.

## Evaluating generative models in high energy physics

[Abstract; sections IV-VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.10295). Different metrics have different sensitivities; an interpretable observable and a multivariate score provide complementary evidence.

Application: Connect the observed differences to complementary validation observables; do not turn agreement in a mean or a failed-control classifier score into fidelity.

## ePIC SiPM-on-tile ZDC design

[Abstract; design; performance; summary](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12877v2). The detector study evaluates reconstructed neutron energy and angle using hit energies and positions. It explicitly separates demonstrated performance from prospective timing work.

Application: Name neutron energy and angle as the downstream questions, while stating that the present graph counts do not measure either and the production geometry is not verified equivalent.

## CaloDREAM

[Abstract; classifier discussion; section 5 Outlook](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09629v3). Low-energy deposits, sparsity and thresholds can affect comparisons. Generalization to irregular geometry, hadrons and varying angles requires further study.

Application: Keep the zero-threshold definition adjacent to the findings; preserve unmeasured threshold/graph sensitivity and numerical-solver limits.

## ALICE normalizing-flow/co-activation study

[Abstract; conditional/co-activation results; bootstrap discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12795). The study uses conditional response and co-activation observables with explicit resampling units and uncertainty limitations.

Application: State the reused validation bank, separate nonempty samples and absence of paired structural intervals; distinguish descriptive differences from a significance or causal claim.
