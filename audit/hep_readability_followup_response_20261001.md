The new audit correctly emphasizes physical interpretation, but its claim of multiple severe math/typesetting faults is based on distorted PDF extraction. In the checked `main.tex` and rendered pages 2, 4–7 and 13, Eq. 1 has its commas and unit spacing; the channel target is nonnegative; Eqs. 2, 7, 8, 9–12 retain their symbols and correct indices; Eq. 12 has both membership cases; $m\geq R$ and generator-minus-reference are printed correctly; and the alleged table typos are absent. These forms were already covered by the previous release QA. No mathematical equation or numeric table was changed in this pass.

The accepted edits make the ePIC design connection more direct, define $\mathbf p$, explain the abstract's condition-only control without guessing its cause, replace “diagnostic note” with a precise study description, show an illustrative two-section calibration identity, and explain that thresholding might shorten a low-energy tail while creating new interior gaps. The [ePIC design study](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12877v2) supports the position-sensitive reconstruction context and documents its own 0.5-MIP and 275-ns choices; the [PDG calorimeter review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-particle-detectors-accel.pdf) supports the general need to consider sampling and response calibration. Neither source establishes those settings or coefficients for this production sample.

We retain the manuscript's explicit uncertainty about zero-deposit events, detector materials and coordinate frame. The component bound proves extra graph disconnection within contiguous active-layer segments, including possible failures across adjacent occupied layers; it does not prove an 84.6% *purely lateral* contribution. The 0.464 condition-only AUROC is compatible with pair-splitting bias, but the causal mechanism remains unverified. The 17-MeV fixed deep-region mean does not give the energy of additional late hits, and the 0.243-s residual is not generator latency. The suggested autoregressive/spatial models are useful controlled tests, not experimentally established fixes. Exact input/source hashes and itemized dispositions are in the JSON twin.
