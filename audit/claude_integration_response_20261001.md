# Final disposition of the independent reader proposal

The complete 18-page proposal and supplied fresh-reader audit were read. The final revision integrates their supported improvements into the existing manuscript. The proposal remains preserved under `proposals/claude_20261001/` as review evidence, not as the current release. The author's request authorizes updating the linked GitHub repository.

## Adopted

- Physics motivation now starts from the ZDC measurement of neutral particles close to the outgoing hadron beam and the use of hit energies and positions in neutron reconstruction. Checked directly against the primary design study, https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12877v2 . Its detector is context; its material stack is not silently assigned to this sample.
- Measured stored-coordinate dimensions: 30.3 mm ECAL horizontal centroid pitch, 24.9 mm median HCAL layer z spacing, 1.57 m first-to-last HCAL mean-z span, and ECAL mean z of 35.73 m. These values are recomputed from the paper's geometry. No claim identifies the coordinate origin with the interaction point or equates a stored-ID centroid with a physical tile.
- The proposed all-layer geometry view replaces the redundant multiplicity histogram. The proposed gap/run/component schematic replaces the plot of algebraic interval endpoints; all numerical bounds remain in Results and Discussion. Each schematic count is independently checked, and its graph is explicitly illustrative.
- Model-stage labels and clearer centered-log-fraction language improve navigation while retaining all fourteen original equations. The physical meaning and dependencies of response, layer activity, energy budgets, channel multiplicity, placement and energy sharing remain explicit. The existing diagram and stage prose provide the overview without another dense table.
- The abstract now states the mean-agreement versus shower-structure question directly. The within-run result is stated in Results as well as Discussion. The claim that data were merely not recovered locally is replaced by the actual analysis boundary: the summary lacks per-event masks and energies, and the remaining studies require an event-level evaluation.
- The existing paired normalized-response mean is reported: 2.935218e-5, interval [-8.513054e-4, 9.337961e-4]. This interval includes zero and applies to (T_gen-T_ref)/K_inc, not the unnormalized mean difference or each energy bin.
- Layers 57--64 carry 17.19219 and 17.84933 MeV per event in reference and generated samples, respectively. The manuscript explains what that fixed-region mean does and does not measure.
- Total evaluation and disjoint timed-analysis durations are reported, with their residual attributed to generation, I/O and other untimed work. The archived StageTimer implementation shows separate, nonnested measurements. The residual is not presented as generation latency or a speed-up benchmark.
- October title/release dates and an explicit Anthropic Claude acknowledgment update the author disclosure. No author affiliation was added.

## Not adopted as scientific conclusions

1. **Uncorrelated pairing from a normalized RMS.** A single moment E[(T_gen-T_ref)^2/K^2] does not determine E[(T_gen-T_ref)^2], or their paired covariance. Weighted covariance contributions can cancel while unweighted contributions do not: equal populations at K=1 and K=2 with contributions 1 and -4 have zero average contribution after division by K^2, but an unweighted average of -1.5. Bin-center/uniform-energy approximations and numerical agreement at three decimals do not establish zero covariance. Thus the 0.7-sigma claim, independent-bin chi-square and nonsignificance of the unnormalized mean were not adopted. The directly computed paired interval is used for its actual estimand.
2. **Extra depth carries little energy.** The mean energy in a fixed depth interval is supported; the energy specifically associated with additional occupied layers, conditional on differing event masks, is not. It does not locate disconnected components or establish threshold robustness. Those stronger claims were removed.
3. **Physical detector identity and acceptance.** Similar dimensions and an approximately -25 mrad coordinate guide do not verify the production material stack, coordinate origin, beam axis or nuclear interaction length. Consequently, exp(-7) cannot establish this sample's empty-shower mechanism. No inferred punch-through probability or acceptance explanation was adopted.
4. **Classifier lower bounds and mechanism.** Population-optimal classification and finite, reused-sample AUROC are different quantities. Failed controls, statistical uncertainty and row-split pair dependence prevent interpreting every measured score as a certified lower bound. Lower channel-score AUROC also does not prove that the classifier ignored all channel-level information. Both original and separate-monitor values remain scoped to their banks; the test failure and control failure remain explicit.
5. **Overfitting onset and independent robustness.** A noisy minimum in a continued, restarted training lineage does not by itself locate the onset of overfitting. Training and validation objectives are evaluated differently. Descendant checkpoints are not independent seeds; heterogeneous modifications, incomplete provenance checks and failed screens cannot be recast as a controlled causal test. Their historical evidence is preserved but not promoted into the one-checkpoint result.
6. **Reconstruction and remote experiments.** The manuscript now says this study does not measure the graph discrepancy's reconstruction effect, avoiding a project-wide claim that no proxy exists. Reports of remote checkpoints and trained activity alternatives identify follow-up work; no remote run or new test use was launched under a manuscript-finalization request. Decoder threshold arguments are not permission to change the frozen target: a postprocessing sensitivity study would need a specified event-level protocol.
7. **Additional distances and curves.** Existing half-split distances are finite-sample context, not critical values, guaranteed noise ceilings or significance calibrations. The proposal's larger distance table and training plot remain in the preserved review, while the final manuscript retains four figures with distinct explanatory purposes. Numerical evidence was not removed to hide unfavorable results; the full report and history remain available.

## Validation and publication scope

The 14-page limit, original-equation and primary-table preservation, full numerical checks, historical-failure records, and exact source/PDF/figure/visual binding remain enforced. No assertions, thresholds or frozen configurations were relaxed. The peer's arithmetic-check count is not used as proof of its statistical interpretations. The active QA series records all integration attempts, including failures and corrections.

The manuscript is an aggregate diagnostic arXiv note. Empirical threshold sensitivity, exact component/run distributions, same-bank pair-grouped classification, independent fits and detector-level reconstruction remain unfinished research. Local finalization and Git publication do not claim that those experiments were performed or that arXiv server compilation has been verified.
