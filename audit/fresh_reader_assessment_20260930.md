# Fresh-reader assessment: HEP and computational-physics manuscript

## Overall judgment

The manuscript has a coherent purpose and a useful descriptive result: mean energy and occupancy can look similar while occupied layers extend farther downstream and contain more gaps. It explains the selected generator well enough to follow that observation and to see which generation stages control which observables. It does **not** provide a complete reproducible training experiment or establish a validated fast simulator. Its treatment of uncertainty, detector provenance, conditional fidelity, architecture comparisons and runtime is necessarily incomplete because the required evidence is missing.

The appropriate answer is therefore qualified: the revised manuscript works as a pilot diagnostic case study for an experimental HEP/computational-physics audience. Some scientific purposes remain unfulfilled, even when their limitations are clearly disclosed. More rereading cannot substitute for the missing measurements.

This assessment concerns the local v0.16.0 working revision of `main.tex`, identified by the exact source and PDF hashes in the accompanying review and final-build audits. It is an assistant review of the complete paper, not independent human peer review. Earlier reviews were consulted for evidence identity and build procedures, not adopted as verdicts. No raw data, new test sample, training run or model evaluation was used.

## Mathematics: why every displayed equation is present

All fourteen displayed equation blocks remain byte-for-byte identical to the pre-review source after newline normalization. Prose around them was revised where it left avoidable ambiguity. Their inclusion is justified as follows.

| Equation | Purpose and connection to this experiment | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1, conditioning | Maps the neutron four-vector into the five inputs used by every learned stage; distinguishes total and kinetic energy | It does not prove whether the production four-vector was recorded at generation or detector entry |
| 2, visibility and mixture | Separates the empty-readout hurdle from the continuous transformed response distribution | The displayed Gaussian mixture has real support; it is not by itself a positive physical energy distribution |
| 3, clipping and cap | Specifies how a mixture draw becomes the actual sampled total, including extra routes to an empty event | The cap is a training-derived numerical convention; tail distortion is unmeasured |
| 4, activity factorization | Exposes how the model chooses later active layers and why gaps are possible | Conditional factorization does not establish that it caused the observed gap excess; shared upstream variables can still correlate layers marginally |
| 5, profile target | Shows how observed layer energies become the flow-training target | Centering removes a common offset; flooring first modifies sufficiently small original fractions. The new prose states this |
| 6, flow objective | Defines the supervised vector-field target and the active-coordinate loss normalization | A lower training loss is not a detector-fidelity or free-running-support metric |
| 7, generation updates | States the actual eight-update numerical sampler | Midpoint time evaluation with the current state is not a demonstrated second-order midpoint-state solver or a convergence study |
| 8, layer budgets | Shows exactly how active-layer logits become nonnegative shares summing to sampled total | It guarantees sampled-budget accounting, not equality to incident energy or the reference shower |
| 9, graph messages | Explains how readout geometry can inform channel scores and fields | The graph is an adjacency hypothesis, not a microscopic shower-transport simulation |
| 10, top-k support | Makes fixed cardinality and stochastic channel choice explicit | Connectivity is unconstrained; clipped random variates are a numerical implementation detail |
| 11, channel target | Gives the second flow's reference target on the positive reference support | The shared notation x1 is explicitly distinguished from the layer target; floor effects still apply |
| 12, decoder | Completes the path to 6,790 channel energies and proves exact-arithmetic budget identities | Floating-point behavior must be checked separately; it cannot repair the chosen support |
| 13, nine-term objective | Relates the sampling decisions to the optimization and selected checkpoint | A weighted teacher-forced objective is not the same quantity as any plotted shower diagnostic |
| 14, gap count | Defines the central observable and connects span, active-layer count and gaps | It counts individual empty layers, not empty runs, weak components, leakage or reconstructed clusters |

No unnecessary new display equation was added. The example of occupied layers {1,2,5} versus {1,3,5} is an algebraic illustration, not simulated or measured physics evidence. Both have G=2 but their occupied-run counts are two and three, respectively.

## Figures and tables: purpose, explanation and limitations

**Figure 1, readout geometry.** Necessary to explain that the node represents a readout ID and sometimes a centroid of several positions. The ECAL/HCAL examples and full ganging histogram support that explanation. Counts, coordinate units, color scale and first-bin composition are legible. It is not a complete detector-material or longitudinal-layout drawing. The caption now says why this matters to the shared model/diagnostic graph.

**Figure 2, architecture.** Necessary because a reader should not need to reconstruct the cascade from fourteen equations. The serpentine order is stated; stochastic flows and deterministic decoding are distinct. The caption now makes clear that the arrows omit some conditioning links. The original colors were correct. No false-positive color complaint from an earlier review was repeated. The diagram gives stages; graph-block and attention details appropriately remain in adjacent prose.

**Figure 3, deposited-energy profile.** Necessary because the similar total means partly result from opposing ECAL/HCAL shifts. Linear and log views expose different parts of the HCAL mean profile. It is intentionally not evidence about per-event gaps. The revised caption states compensation and the mean-profile limitation while retaining sample size, empty events, layer ranges and line identities. No uncertainty bands can be supplied from the available evidence.

**Figure 4, support summary.** Useful despite overlap with Table 1: its job is to place nearly equal active-layer means beside markedly different last-layer/gap/component means. Zero-based axes and explicit differences prevent ratio-only framing. The caption now discloses unavailable uncertainty intervals locally. The bars remain descriptive point estimates and should not be read as significance statements. An eventual paired gap/run distribution would be more informative, but cannot be invented from these aggregates.

**Table 1.** The main numeric comparison is necessary and unchanged. Different nonempty denominators, percentage points and pre-rounding subtraction are explicit. Small apparent discrepancies from subtracting rounded printed entries are not arithmetic defects.

**Table 2.** The calibration proposal documents a retained procedure, with a prominent caveat that applied weights could not be independently checked. Mean-one normalization after clipping explains values outside the pre-normalization clipping interval. It is not evidence of an optimal weighting strategy.

**Table 3.** Needed to show that the 1% overall mean difference conceals opposing bin-level differences. Counts sum to 10,000. Bin closure, units and mean/standard-deviation definitions are supplied. Uncertainties remain unavailable.

**Table 4.** Needed to disclose every classifier seed and the failed high-level threshold. The seeds vary the classifier/row split, not independently trained generators. It must not be used as three-seed evidence for the generator.

## Scientific work that editing cannot complete

1. **Quantify the central descriptive result.** Recover the permitted event-level validation bank and estimate paired, energy-stratified intervals for gap prevalence, mean gaps, last layer, run count and component metrics. Recompute the separate nonempty denominator in each resample. Preserve pairing before applying selections. These would quantify bank-level sampling uncertainty, not erase repeated-inspection bias or substitute for independent fits.
2. **Test whether the support difference survives meaningful definitions.** Perform explicitly specified diagnostic threshold scans while retaining raw deposited energy as the production target. Compare graph constructions independently of the generator's own graph, and examine energy/direction slices. Report component locations and energies to separate tiny isolated deposits from energy-carrying fragmentation.
3. **Separate longitudinal gaps from connectivity.** Retain occupied-run counts and compare components within runs. The aggregates do not justify subtracting mean gap counts from mean components or interpreting their difference as pure lateral fragmentation.
4. **Complete statistical design before a stronger fidelity claim.** Freeze evaluation identities, group matched conditions in C2ST, repeat generation at fixed conditions and report at least three independent final model fits. Existing classifier seeds do not fill this role. A chance condition-only score does not establish shower fidelity.
5. **Recover executable provenance.** Obtain the actual historical configuration, complete stage/checkpoint lineage, environment, detector production settings and simulation-job grouping if available. Mark missing fields as unknown. Source hashes alone do not recreate the environment.
6. **Only then evaluate broader model utility.** Use matched baselines, oracle-cascade diagnostics, cap/solver sensitivity, reconstruction studies and end-to-end timing for architecture, causality, detector-performance or speed claims. The present paper should not suggest those outcomes have already been measured.

These are evidence requirements for stronger claims, not permissions to access protected data or launch experiments. No such work was launched during this review.

## Review method and stopping rule

Readings cover the entire manuscript in its existing order, including declarations and appendices. Full readings and focused checks are distinguished in the JSON audit. The repeated questions are: What is this part for? Is its input population and output quantity clear? Can the reader connect its mathematics to the detector target? What inference does it support? What could a skeptical reader reasonably misunderstand? Does the next section use only what has been established?

Focused checks revisit: terminology, units, conditional/marginal distinctions, exact versus numerical identities, graph confounding, figure purpose, sample accounting, numerical claims, provenance, literature scope, layout and conclusion strength. Re-running a build is not counted as a full reading. Re-reading is not counted as independent review or additional physics evidence.

The practical stopping condition is a complete final reading with no new actionable editorial inconsistency, successful unchanged repository checks, and direct inspection of every final page. Missing scientific evidence remains listed above; a large number of passes cannot turn that absence into a pass.
